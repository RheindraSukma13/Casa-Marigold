Our Story

Built around one wood-fired grill.

Casa Marigold started small, and stayed small on purpose. Here's the short version of how we got here.

Casa Marigold opened with one grill, four tables, and a menu built entirely around live fire. The idea was simple: cook the way Mexican grandmothers have cooked for generations, but give it a room worth dressing up for.

Every tortilla is pressed by hand. Every mole takes two days. We work with a handful of small farms for our corn and chiles, and we change the menu with what's actually in season — not what's easy to keep in stock.

What makes us different isn't a secret ingredient. It's that we never scaled past the point where the kitchen could still taste everything before it left the pass.
